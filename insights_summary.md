# Customer Lifetime Value — Insights Summary

## Key Findings
- Customer-level metrics were calculated from charging-session records.
- Customers were ranked using their observed total spending.
- Customers were grouped into Low, Medium, and High Value segments.
- An annualized spending estimate was calculated using observed session activity.

## Business Recommendations
1. Prioritize high-spending customers for loyalty programs.
2. Encourage repeat charging through targeted offers.
3. Monitor customer activity to identify declining engagement.
4. Evaluate retention and profitability before allocating marketing budgets.

## Important Note
These findings describe historical spending and usage. Annualized spending should not be interpreted as a validated prediction of future customer lifetime value.

- Customer-level CLV results in CSV and Excel formats.
