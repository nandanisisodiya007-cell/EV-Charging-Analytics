3. Recommendations

- Investigate infrastructure capacity at stations with high total energy consumption.
- Review hourly charging patterns to understand when energy demand is concentrated.
- Compare regional consumption with charger availability and station capacity before making expansion decisions.
- Use the daily energy trend as a starting point for developing and validating a more robust forecasting model.
- Calculate overlapping charging intervals and power demand to assess actual grid stress.

4. Limitations

The peak hour represents total energy associated with sessions starting during that hour. It does not necessarily represent instantaneous grid load or peak electrical power.

The rolling-average estimate is a basic trend reference and has not been validated against a separate test period.

5. Conclusion

The dataset shows differences in energy consumption across stations and regions. These results can guide further investigation into infrastructure planning, charging schedules, and regional energy needs.

The findings should be combined with charger capacity, overlapping sessions, and local grid data before operational decisions are made.
